CHAPTER IV

When the Moon Refused to Sleep

Beyond the stars where silence gleams,
There lives the gentle home of dreams.
A place untouched by fear or time,
Where every hope becomes a rhyme.

The moon still guards each wish we keep,
Awakening hearts from restless sleep.
Perhaps the end is not goodbye,
But one more dawn beyond the sky.

Tell me...

Will you awaken to the light,
Or dream forever through the night?

Awaken Dream Forever
