CHAPTER I

The Stars We Named Forever

Love arrived with silent grace,
A fleeting light, a warm embrace.
It bloomed beneath a moonlit sky,
Yet whispered softly, "All must die."

Your eyes became my brightest star,
A distant glow, yet never far.
I chased the dawn with hopeful sight,
Only to lose you with the night.

Now every page and every dove
Still speaks the fragile name of love.

Tell me...

Will you keep what time has torn,
Or leave behind what can't return?
