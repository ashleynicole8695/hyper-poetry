CHAPTER III

Echoes Beneath the Rain

The rain recalls forgotten days,
Its gentle grief, its endless gaze.
Each drop repeats what words concealed,
Each wound reminds what never healed.

I searched for you through mist and stone,
Yet found the road was mine alone.
Still every echo softly sings,
Of all the weight that memory brings.

Tell me...

Will you forgive the past at last,
Or let its shadow always last?

Forgive Keep Remembering
