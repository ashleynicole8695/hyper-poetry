CHAPTER II

Where Morning Learns to Bloom

Though storms may steal the light away,
The sun still finds another day.
From broken roots, new blossoms grow,
Where silent streams of courage flow.

The heart may bend beneath the rain,
Yet hope still softens every pain.
For every dusk that veils the sky,
A brighter dawn is drawing nigh.

Tell me...

Will you follow morning's light,
Or wander through the fading night?
